Cookies Policy

How we use cookies

This policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and how you can manage them on E-Learn.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the website remember your preferences, improve performance, and deliver a better experience.

How we use cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential cookies — required for the platform to function (authentication, security, session management).
  • Preference cookies — remember your settings such as theme (dark/light mode) and language.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand how users interact with the platform so we can improve it.

Third-party cookies

We may use third-party services (e.g., payment processors, embedded content) that set their own cookies. These are governed by the respective third-party privacy policies.

Managing cookies

Most browsers allow you to control cookies through your settings. You can block or delete cookies, but doing so may affect the functionality of this platform.

To learn how to manage cookies in your browser, visit:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about our use of cookies, contact us at support@elearn.local.
